# Demo script (about 75 seconds)

Record the loopback page (`hermes flowlight serve`) full screen. Dark room, no desktop chrome. Voice stays under the picture.

| Time | Shot | What the viewer should see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–8s | Terminal | `hermes flowlight watch purefile1686.near and 0xe7030be388b8e247abe0c9fa876673e83fbcbd3c on Base`. Hold on the sentence that names the largest inflow and the explorer link. |
| 8–22s | Wide picture | Cut to the page already drawing. LIVE badge. Cyan USDC arcs pour into the Base hub. The green `purefile1686.near` node sits above it with no arcs. Particles travel along the curves. |
| 22–36s | Detail | Click the thickest arc. Panel shows the single transfer amount, the route total, the time, and **Open transfer in explorer**. |
| 36–50s | Time | Drag the scrubber left. Arcs drop away. Press Play and let them draw back in. |
| 50–64s | Fixture | Click **Demo fixture**. The amber DEMO FIXTURE badge is unmistakable. Green NEAR, purple WETH, and teal AERO arcs join the blue USDC rivers. |
| 64–75s | Back to live | Click **Live**. The real Base hub returns. End on the glowing constellation. One line of voice: "Read-only. No keys. No transactions." |

Do not show a seed phrase, a wallet popup, or a send button. There isn't one.
